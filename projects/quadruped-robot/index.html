Four-legged walking robot

An organic-looking quadruped, built to walk on eight servos and a $100–150 parts budget.

Context
Columbia Robotics Studio
Status
In progress
Focus
Legged robotics, servo actuation
Fabrication
3D-printed shell and textures

Problem

Build a four-legged robot that walks and looks organic rather than mechanical, inside a fixed hardware and cost envelope.

Constraints

  • ActuatorsMax 8 serial-bus servos, 240° range
  • ControllerSingle-board computer
  • PowerRechargeable battery
  • Budget~$100–150 in parts
  • Body3D-printed shell and textures

Design decisions

Actuation

Serial-bus servos with 240° of travel, capped at eight for the whole robot.

Control

A single-board computer runs the robot.

Form

A 3D-printed shell with printed surface textures gives the robot its organic look.

Build

Build log in progress.
