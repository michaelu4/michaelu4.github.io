Dual-Axis Solar Tracker

Spring 2026 · MechE Lab II

Four of us, assigned at random, had one semester to build a tracker that turns a solar panel toward the brightest light. I owned the code and electronics. For weeks the tracker hunted around instead of locking on. The sensor readings told me why: stray light was leaking onto the photoresistors. Shielding each sensor with a 3D-printed sleeve and teaching the program to calibrate to the room made it find the light 30% faster. Alternating lab weeks with build weeks helped, because each experiment fed directly into the next round of fixes.
